Third-party components and inspirations
---------------------------------------

  - pokemon-colorscripts by phoneybadger
    https://gitlab.com/phoneybadger/pokemon-colorscripts
    (Concept of "art in your terminal on every session" — the spark
    that made this project possible.)

  - chafa by Hans Petter Jansson
    https://hpjansson.org/chafa/
    (Universal terminal image rendering. Used at runtime for image
    display and in the contributor pipeline for ANSI generation.)

  - fastfetch
    https://github.com/fastfetch-cli/fastfetch
    (System info alongside the car / pokemon at terminal startup.)

  - rembg by Daniel Gatis
    https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg
    (Background removal in the contributor asset pipeline.)

  - Wikimedia Commons and its contributors
    (Source of every image bundled with this project. This project
    is a curation on top of their generous work.)

Thank you to everyone who contributes, forks, and shares this
project. Automotive terminal art deserves a place in the ricing
community, and none of it would exist without the free-culture
photographers whose work fills `assets/`.
